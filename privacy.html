Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Lucida is a photo browser and culling app for the Mac, made by one developer. The whole policy fits in a sentence: Lucida doesn't collect your data. The details are below.

Everything stays on your Mac

Lucida reads your photos straight from the folders you pick. It never copies, uploads, or alters them. Your ratings, flags, color labels, keywords, the people you've named, smart albums, collage templates, and everything the app works out on its own (sharpness, tone, scene type, faces, the machine score, similar shots) live in a database inside Lucida's own sandbox container on your Mac. Thumbnail caches live there too. Library backups are ordinary files that you create and keep wherever you like.

What can leave your Mac, and only when you ask

  • Place names. Turn on Resolve place names in Settings and Lucida sends the GPS coordinates from your photos, rounded to a grid about two kilometers wide, to Apple's geocoding service, which returns a country and city. Nothing else is sent. This is off by default.
  • Your own model server. If you point Settings › Smart Classification at an HTTP model server, Lucida sends downsized photos or face crops to that address, and asks you before it does. The server is yours; Lucida has none of its own. This is off by default, and the built-in classifier runs entirely on your Mac.
  • Sharing, exporting, printing. Anything you share, export, or print goes exactly where you send it, through the standard macOS share sheet, save dialogs, and print system.
  • The App Store. Apple handles the Lucida Pro purchase. Lucida never sees your payment details.

What I collect

Nothing. No analytics, no crash reports, no accounts, no ads. Apart from the two optional features above, Lucida makes no network connections at all.

Permissions

Lucida runs inside the macOS sandbox and can only read the folders you add to its sidebar, through the security-scoped bookmarks you grant. Moving a file to the Trash or writing an XMP sidecar needs write access to that folder, so Lucida asks first. You can revoke that access at any time in Settings › General.

Children

Lucida isn't aimed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new date. Questions? See the support page.
